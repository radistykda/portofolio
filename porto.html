About

The planner and the performer

I'm a Software Engineering student in the International Class Program at SMK Telkom Malang, where I've learned to treat problems — technical or logistical — the same way: break them into components, find the workflow, and automate what shouldn't be manual. That systems thinking now runs quietly behind everything I do outside the classroom, from the Notion pipelines I built to keep Media Moklet's content calendar on schedule to the run-of-show documents I write before every event I lead.

But structure only gets an event halfway there — the rest is voice. As a national-level debater, bilingual MC, and English coach, I've spent the last two years learning how to hold a room, translate ideas across languages on the fly, and keep a program moving even when the schedule doesn't cooperate. I'm applying to the Diesnat committee because I want to bring both halves of that skill set — the planner and the performer — to one of Moklet's biggest stages.
